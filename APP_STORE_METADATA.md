## App Store Description

Sleft Golf is the social network built for golfers who want to play more, connect with playing partners, and track their game — all in one place.

**POST YOUR ROUNDS**
Shot a personal best? Had a rough day on the links? Share your round in seconds with course, score, and vibe. Your friends see it in the feed instantly.

**FIND PLAYING PARTNERS**
Browse open tee times, join groups, or post your own. The Board shows every upcoming round at a glance — tap to join, and you're in.

**AI CADDIE SCHEDULING**
Tell the Caddie when and where you want to play in plain English. It handles the rest — finds courses, suggests times, and gets you on the course faster.

**RATE YOUR PLAYING PARTNERS**
After a round, rate pace of play, etiquette, and how fun it was to play together. Build your reputation as a great playing partner.

**GROUP CHAT**
Every tee time has its own chat room. Coordinate logistics, talk trash, or share the forecast — all in one place.

**TRACK YOUR GAME**
See your stats: total rounds, average score, best score, and handicap. Your profile tells your golf story.
